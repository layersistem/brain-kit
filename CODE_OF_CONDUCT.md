# Code of Conduct

This project is maintained by a small team and used by people who put their own notes and working memory into it. Every issue, pull request and discussion here should be a place where a stranger can report a bug, ask a question or disagree with a design without being talked down to.

## Expectations

- Be direct and be kind. Say what is wrong and what you measured; do not say what you assume about the person.
- Assume the other side is acting in good faith until the evidence says otherwise.
- Keep disagreement on the substance: the code, the measurement, the design. A review that says "this is wrong because X" is welcome; one that says "you should know better" is not.
- Respect people's time. Search before filing, quote the exact error, and say what you already tried.
- Never post someone else's private data. Transcripts and vault contents are personal by nature; redact them before pasting.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, sexualised language, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of a thread after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Report a problem to info@layersistem.com, or use GitHub's report feature on the comment itself. Reports are read by the maintainers, handled in confidence, and answered. A maintainer who is the subject of a report does not take part in handling it.

## Consequences

Depending on severity: a private note, a public correction in the thread, removal of the content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the repository. The maintainers hold themselves to the same standard.
